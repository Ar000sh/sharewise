How a balance is made

Balance = what you paid − your share of every expense. A positive balance means the group owes you; a negative one means you owe the group.

Only confirmed payments change a balance. A reported payment waits until the other person answers.

Right after you add an expense the balances show “Updating…” for a moment before they catch up.
